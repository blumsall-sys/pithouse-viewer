Controls

Desktop: WASD / arrows walk, Space jump, mouse look, E interact, I inventory, Esc / P pause or back, 1–3 choose dialogue, Enter reveal text, ~ debug.

USB gamepad: left stick walk, right stick look, X jump, A interact/confirm (jump when no target), B back, Y inventory, Start pause, D-pad dialogue/menu navigation.

Mobile: drag left pad to move and right pad to look. Tap Interact or the floating markers. Screen buttons are sized for landscape.

The Elder is a stand-in until you provide a rigged GLB and named animation clips.
