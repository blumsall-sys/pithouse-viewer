Controls

Desktop: WASD / arrow keys move, mouse drag or click to lock pointer and look, E interact, 1–3 choose a response, Esc release mouse, ~ toggle debug view.

Mobile: drag left pad to move and right pad to look. Tap Interact or the floating markers. Screen buttons are sized for landscape.

The Elder is a stand-in until you provide a rigged GLB and named animation clips.
